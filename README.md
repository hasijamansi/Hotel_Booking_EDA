# Hotel Booking EDA

## Project Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on hotel booking data from INN Hotels Group.

The objective is to understand booking patterns, customer characteristics, room pricing, and factors associated with booking cancellations.

## Objectives

- Explore the structure and characteristics of the hotel booking dataset.
- Analyse numerical and categorical variables.
- Identify monthly booking patterns.
- Analyse booking cancellation behavior.
- Examine the relationship between special requests and cancellations.
- Compare room prices across different market segments.
- Identify outliers and important relationships between variables.

## Key Findings

- October has the highest number of bookings, followed by September and August.
- The Online market segment contributes the largest number of bookings.
- Approximately 32.76% of bookings are canceled.
- Approximately 1.72% of repeating guests cancel.
- Bookings with special requests generally have a lower cancellation proportion.
- Lead time is positively associated with booking cancellation.
- Room prices vary across market segments.
- Outliers were retained because they may represent genuine booking or pricing patterns.

The analysis identifies important patterns in booking volume, cancellation behavior, market segments, lead time, room pricing, and guest requirements. These findings can support further predictive modeling and data-driven hotel cancellation and refund strategies.
